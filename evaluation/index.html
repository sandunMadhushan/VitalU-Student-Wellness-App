What students get in VitalU

One app for the checks, trackers and guidance a busy undergraduate actually uses. Results stay private to the student.

Know your numbers

BMI and waist-to-hip ratio with plain-language meaning, a heart health check, and a blood report reader.

Everyday trackers

Water, steps, active minutes, sleep and the menstrual cycle, each logged in a tap or two.

Eat well on a budget

Calorie counter, meal diary, healthy plate, dietary diversity guide and a budget planner built on Sri Lankan foods.

Mind and learning

The DASS-21 anxiety tracker with calm music and relaxation ideas, plus short health articles written for students.

Help when you need it

Ask the health centre anonymously, find campus professionals, and reach emergency numbers quickly.

Badges for good habits

Aqua Achiever, Active Aura, Smart Eater, Heart Minded and Curious Learner celebrate streaks without pressure.

Every six months: a health check-in

Each faculty gets a one-week window for the periodic health assessment. It takes about seven minutes, and answers are pooled anonymously so the university can plan health and nutrition support.

For the university: a pooled dashboard

Administration schedules assessments by faculty and sees only group results. Groups smaller than five are hidden, and no individual's answers are ever shown.

Tell us how the pilot went

Your answers shape the next version of VitalU. The form is anonymous: no name, email or registration number. It takes 4 to 8 minutes, and you can skip any question.

The research behind VitalU

"Development of an ADDIE-Based Mobile Health Platform to Improve Cardiometabolic Health among Undergraduate Students"

The platform supports the cardiometabolic health and overall well-being of undergraduates through personalised nutrition guidance, dietary assessment, health monitoring and evidence-based lifestyle recommendations.
